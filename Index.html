10. Hakugei

Hakugei is and RMC coaster located in Nagashima Spa Land in Kuwana, Japan. It is a hybrid coaster meaning it is made of wood and steel. It has a height of 180 feet, is 5020 feet long, and hits speeds of up to 66 mph. It feutures 3 inversions, (a zero G stall and two zero G rolls) a first drop height of approximately 160-170 feet at 80 degrees,
